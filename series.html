#ModelvrsMannequin

Model Vrs Mannequin is me versus the mannequins.

Recording myself dancing alone just felt kind of silly, so I brought backup: the mannequins. If it’s me versus the mannequins, I always win, because they don’t move. And if people hate? “At least I’m better than the mannequin.”

I also wonder if they come alive while I’m not around. Some days, standing perfectly still, they still win.

Every video is an outfit of the day, usually danced in heels, to show curvy girls they can have “a little cute outfit” too.
