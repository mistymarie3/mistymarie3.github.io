About Misty

Actor first. Model, mover, maker.

I’m a SAG-AFTRA actor and model based in Los Angeles. I started ballet at four and took my first acting classes at Seattle Children’s Theatre at five, from clowning and musical theater to comedy and accents. Jazz, tap and hip-hop came next, and I’ve been performing ever since. I studied Acting for the Camera at UNLV, spent a semester in France studying French and dance, and trained in improv at The Groundlings and Second City and in audition technique at BGB. You may have seen me on HBO’s Insecure, Netflix’s Huge in France or ABC’s Rebel.

Modeling found me by accident. A friend told me about a plus-size casting. I got a free walking lesson at a Beverly Hills mall, tagged along with her to the casting, and booked the show. More than ten years later, I’m a fit model for major fashion brands at home and overseas in China, I’ve walked runways in California, Seattle and New York, and I coach new models on their walk and their confidence, keeping up as runway styles change. I also figure skate, style, and write, direct and produce my own projects.
