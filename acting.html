On Screen

A SAG-AFTRA actor with a BFA in Theatre (Acting for the Camera) from UNLV, Misty has appeared on HBO’s Insecure, Netflix’s Huge in France and ABC’s Rebel. Equally at home in comedy and drama, she brings improv instincts and classical training to every role.
